Eternal Tree
Design Study
Cosmic Ledger

Indigo void over a star-strewn canvas. CJK sigils on circular medallions, edges curve like ley lines between souls. Karma is the single gold accent; everything else is silver and violet. (This is what is shipped on main today.)

Same 7 nodes, same edges, same state distribution in every variant: 道 / 晶 / 儉 anchored, 眼 available next, 星 / 響 / 長 still bound. Two edges are lit, three are mid-anchor, one is fully locked.

Spark Picker, Plaque Pair mockup

Two vertical wood plaques hanging from cords above a stone altar. Same visual family as the home screen Heavenly Qi tablet (kicker, glyph, hairline separators, ember motes), scaled up so two fit side by side on a 390 px viewport.

Card content is bare essentials only: rarity kicker, large icon (the glyph slot), name in Cinzel, single effect line, Pick button. Lore and examples are dropped from the picker entirely. The full story lives on the Sparks tab once a card has been picked.

Locked at 160 px wide × 320 px tall regardless of effect length. Common, Uncommon, Legendary share frame geometry; only color, glow, and breathing differ. Pick button sits below the plaque, not on it, so the wood reads as inscribed and the action feels like commitment.

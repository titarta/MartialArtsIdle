How this maps to the codebase

Entry point. The existing TopBar Blood Lotus button at src/components/TopBar.jsx:25 opens BloodLotusShopModal today. Change that handler to open the Pavilion by default and demote the IAP modal to a "+" button at the top of the Pavilion card. Today's shop becomes "buy more lotus"; the Pavilion becomes "spend lotus".
Tab 1 · Today's Bounty. Daily rotation, 4 slots, 1 purchase each, reset 04:00 local. Stock seed derives from YYYY-MM-DD + userId hash so the same player sees the same items per-day across devices. Each slot has a category (catalyst / cosmetic / mystery / wildcard) and the pool per category is what you tune.
Tab 2 · Lotus Forge. Permanent unlocks. Halos render on a new VFX layer around the cultivator on Home — sits next to .home-fighter-stage.stage-boosted::before (the existing cf-rung-* aura system) without fighting it. Tier halos gate on realm reached, not on Blood Lotus alone — protects the "earned" feel. Seasonal halo is the time-limited FOMO slot.
Tab 3 · Refinement. Activates reroll_minor at bloodLotus.js:79 (dead code today) and adds Dao Reset Stone + Spark Banking. The major-realm Law Reroll already exists in useLawOffers.js:140 and lives here for discoverability — disabled outside breakthrough windows.

F2P economy sanity check

  • Daily income (existing): ~14 BL/day average from dailyBonus.js:13
  • Rebirth bonus (existing): +50 BL/life when al_4 perk owned
  • Daily Bounty cheapest slot: 40 BL → ~3 days of saving for a catalyst
  • First halo (T1 unlock free): immediately satisfying flex
  • T2 halo: 200 BL → ~2 weeks of saving — feels earned, not grindy
  • Seasonal halo: 800 BL → ~2 months base, or a single $4.99 pack — clean monetization moment without paywalling F2P

What the mockup does NOT show yet

  • Halo equip / preview-in-context flow (would live on Home, swap layer in real-time)
  • Mystery Box reveal animation (1-2 second slot-machine reveal — easy CSS keyframe pass)
  • Sound design (each tab has a different ambient lotus chime per pillar)
  • Achievement-gated halos (e.g. "First T10 ascension" earns a free halo nobody can buy)

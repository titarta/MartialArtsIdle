Focus FX — Variant Tests

Pick favourites for each effect. Section 1: tap anywhere in stage to fire a ring. Section 2: gathering loops auto-run — toggle Intense to compare densities. Section 3: tap the crystal.

1 · Tap Circle FX (one-shot per tap)

Fires once per tap at the cursor location. Lives ~500-900ms. Designed to register the input instantly without obscuring the cultivator.

A · Classic Ring

Single cyan ring expands fast, fades. The current convention — tight, clear.
tap & hold anywhere

B · Double Ring

Tight violet inner (200ms) + soft cyan outer (800ms). Two-stage punch.
tap & hold anywhere

C · Triple Ripple

3 rings staggered 80ms apart. Reads as "deep" energy with each tap.
tap & hold anywhere

D · Solid Pulse

Bright white core fading through cyan. Has more "weight" than rings — bigger feedback.
tap & hold anywhere

E · Sigil Ring

Hexagonal cultivation rune rotating outward. Most stylised — feels intentional, ritualistic.
tap & hold anywhere

F · Soft Bloom

No hard ring — pure radial glow. Subtle, never competes with character — but easy to miss.
tap & hold anywhere

G · Real Qi Ring (sprite)

Actual qi_ring.png scales outward. Carries the qi-particle aesthetic, reads as cultivation energy not generic UI.
tap & hold anywhere

H · Ring + Inner Crescent

qi_ring expanding + qi_crescent rotating inside. Layered, ritualistic.
tap & hold anywhere

I · Ring + Swirl Stack

3 qi_ring staggered + a counter-rotating qi_swirl. Most ornate.
tap & hold anywhere

J · Twin Ring (fast + slow)

Two qi_ring at different speeds/sizes. Reads as a quick "tick" plus follow-through ripple.
tap & hold anywhere

2 · Gathering FX (sustained, while focused)

Loops while the player is in focused state. Compare how different size strategies read — uniform vs mixed vs depth-layered.

A · All Small (uniform)

Only qi_orb_small. Baseline — busy and consistent, but flat.
auto-loop

B · All Bright (uniform)

Only qi_orb_bright. Heavy, majestic, fewer overall — but lacks depth.
auto-loop

C · Depth-Layered (small bg + bright fg)

Small/faint orbs spawn far at low opacity, bright orbs spawn closer. Reads as 3D depth.
auto-loop

D · Random Mix (small/med/bright)

Random size per particle, uniform speed. Variety without depth structure.
auto-loop

E · Crescendo (grow toward character)

Particles spawn small, scale UP as they approach. Reads as "qi condensing" — powerful.
auto-loop

F · Inverse (shrink into character)

Particles spawn LARGE at the edges, shrink as they reach the cultivator. Suggests absorption.
auto-loop

G · C + Sparkle Accents

Depth-layered base (your favourite) plus occasional spark_diamond flecks. Adds twinkle without losing structure.
auto-loop

H · E + Wisp Trails

Crescendo base (your other favourite) plus qi_wisp_diagonal streaks for motion lines. Reads as "pulling in".
auto-loop

I · Full Mix (orbs + sparks + droplets)

Every gentle particle type at small scale, all converging. Densest, busiest — "saturated qi field".
auto-loop

3 · Crystal Click Burst (one-shot on tap)

Tap the crystal to fire. The current production is A (loose spark burst). Compare alternatives — heavier impact, more sparkle variety, ringed shockwave.

A · Outward Spark Burst

8 sparks shoot radially outward, fast. Loose, lively — current style baseline.
tap crystal

B · Mix Sparkle (cross + star + diamond)

Varied sparkle shapes radiate outward. Richer detail, less "single-effect" feeling.
tap crystal

C · Ring Shockwave + Center Burst

Outward ring + central spark_burst. Reads as a focused, weighted impact.
tap crystal

D · Bright Flash

Single large white→cyan flash. Heaviest visual weight per tap — most "powerful" feel.
tap crystal

E · Shatter (4 cardinal shards)

Shards fly out N/S/E/W. Crisp, directional — feels like crystal "cracking" energy out.
tap crystal

F · Sustained Charge-Up

tap crystal

tap crystal

H · Qi Orb Explosion

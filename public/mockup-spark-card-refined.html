Spark Picker: Inscribed Cards

A refined take on the picker. Cards read as collected ritual artifacts presented on a temple altar, not as fully painted wood plaques. Card body, content layout, and dimensions stay identical across rarities. Only the frame ornament, accent color, and presence of motion escalate.

Common is intentionally austere: a single bronze hairline border, a tiny paper marker at the top center, a thin rule under the icon. No motion. Uncommon adds jade L-bracket corners, a small green tag at the top, and a faint green halo. Legendary earns its weight with nested gold brackets, a small gold cartouche, a breathing frame glow, gold-leaf shimmer in the body, drifting ember motes, and a red signature seal at the corner. Same skeleton, four layers of escalation.

Picker background is intentionally dark and contemplative: a giant faint 灵 (líng, spirit) watermark hovers behind everything, with two soft incense-light columns placing each card on its own pedestal of light.

Rarity Vocabulary

Border
Common bronze hairline, uncommon jade hairline + green halo, legendary gold hairline with double-stroke breathing glow.
Corners
Common none, uncommon single jade L-bracket, legendary nested double bracket hinting the 回 (huí) motif.
Top marker
Common 3px bronze dot, uncommon jade tag, legendary gold cartouche.
Seal
Common none, uncommon jade tile with 玉, legendary red chop with a character matching the spark (凰 for Phoenix, 龍 for Dragon).
Motion
Common and uncommon static. Legendary breathes a 3.4 second cycle on frame and icon, with ember motes drifting up on a 5.8 second loop.
Hover
Any card lifts 3px on hover. Legendary lifts 5px for extra weight.

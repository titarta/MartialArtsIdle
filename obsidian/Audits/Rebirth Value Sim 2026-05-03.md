## Original headline (for archive)

Three scenarios compared (no rebirth / full tree / rebirth once at idx 24):
- Single rebirth at idx 24 was a net time loss until idx 33 (Origin King 1st).
- Full tree (all 23 nodes, 143 karma) was consistently ~41% faster than no rebirth.
- Marginal value of first rebirth to peak: ~8 days saved (35d → 27d).

**Framework verdict:** Clarity and Motivation both ⚠️ — player couldn't predict the net-loss period; first rebirth felt weak vs qi-time metric alone.

**Tuning suggestions from sim:**
1. Make the first rebirth feel meaningful (bonus early karma or strong early-accessible node).
2. Surface a cost-benefit projection before committing to reincarnate.
3. Link combat softlock relief explicitly to tree perks.

These suggestions informed the 2026-05-24 revamp (continuous karma, accessible tree at Saint realm).

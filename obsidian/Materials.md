## Drop Rules

| Activity | Primary | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Gathering | Herbs | QI stones |
| Mining | Ores | QI stones |
| Combat | Blood cores | QI stones |

**Key rule:** Minerals never drop from combat. Blood cores never drop from gathering/mining.

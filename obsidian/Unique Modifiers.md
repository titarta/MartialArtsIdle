# Unique Modifiers

> **Pools are empty after the 2026-04-24 Damage & Element System Overhaul.** Archive of old ~350 entries: [[Deprecated_Unique_Modifiers]]. New entries must target the 5-element / 2-damage-bucket model — no `essence`/`soul`/`body`, `psychic_damage`, or `soul_toughness`.

Build-enabling rare modifiers for Laws, Artefacts, and Techniques (PoE-uniques / Slormancer-legendaries spirit).

## Pool Power Levels

| Pool | Strength | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Laws | Strongest (often with downsides) | Ascendancy-style build definer |
| Artefacts | ~70% of law strength | Variety, slot-themed buffs |
| Techniques | Specific to technique | Skill specialization |

New pools: `['fire', 'water', 'earth', 'wood', 'metal', 'general']`

## Design Philosophy

1. Builds emerge from 2–5 modifier combinations, not single modifiers
2. Trade-offs create identity (e.g. "100% increased Damage. Cannot heal.")
3. Conditions reward skill expression ("100% increased Damage while below 25% HP")
4. Stat conversions create new playstyles ("30% of Body as Soul")
5. Anti-synergies prevent stacking everything

## Modifier Text Style

- `increased`/`reduced` — additive %
- `more`/`less` — multiplicative %
- Flat numbers — "+8 Phys. Dmg"
- Conditions — `while`, `after`, `on`, `per`

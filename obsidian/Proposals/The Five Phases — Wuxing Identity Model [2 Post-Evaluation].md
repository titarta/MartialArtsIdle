## Monetisation Hooks (proposed)

Spending should be offered at the moment of **desire or near-miss**, not **frustration or block**:

- Close to set completion → "Boost drop rate for matching element 2h"
- Stuck on world boss → "Double material gains 4h"
- Bad modifier roll → "Targeted hone — choose from 3 options"
- Want specific law → "Law Selection Scroll — pick from 3 of your element"
- Approaching pity → "Advance pity by 10"

None of these require changing the Wuxing architecture. They layer on top.

Tap any tab above the phone (or any sub-tab in the rail) to swap the active body. Pavilion plaques use the same framed inset, vermillion rail, and Cinzel lintel as the bazaar — one estate, three rooms.

Before — generic dark cards

Three sub-tabs of identical dark rectangles. The producer name is small sans, the sprite floats with no frame, the buy button is a flat pill, and the locked state shows "??? Locked · Unlocks at realm N" as grey text. Nothing on the screen says place. Nothing tells the player "this Garden, this Furnace, this Pillar — they are yours."

After — The Estate Pavilions

Each producer is a tributary plaque at the pavilion gate. The vermillion rail says qi flows in from here. The framed inset gives the emblem somewhere to live. The Cinzel lintel names the place. Sealed shrines stay visible — players see the wax seal and bamboo lattice and know there’s a destination behind it. When mini-games unlock, the buy cartouche slides left and an “Enter Pavilion” CTA lands in the same slot — the chassis doesn’t change.

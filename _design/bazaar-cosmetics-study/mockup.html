Recommendation

Ship the evolution-procession card for every cosmetic, with the explicit price chip always visible. Drop the "Coming Soon" subsection entirely.

  • One card shape across all cosmetic slots (cultivator / crystal / particles / backdrop). Each renders its own silhouette set: cultivator = 13 forms, crystal = 10 tiers, particles + backdrop = 3 dots/swatches with the tint applied.
  • Price chip never disappears. Buyable: bright vermillion-bordered. Owned-but-not-equipped: muted same chip. Equipped: chip replaced by jade "EQUIPPED" ribbon.
  • CTA simplifies to one verb: Buy / Equip / Unequip. No price text inside the button.
  • Coming Soon section removed. When the real Tier-2 skin assets land, they slot into the same card with their actual silhouettes - no placeholder needed in the meantime.
  • Card matches existing Sanctum vocabulary: lacquer body, brass border, Cinzel display, vermillion-bordered price chip mirroring the TopBar lotus balance.

REFINE button — design study

Four variants. Goal: reads as an UPGRADE (not a generic action), English-first, matches the Sanctum brass/lacquer vocabulary. Each variant shown in both affordable (ready to spend) and unaffordable (insufficient qi) states.
V1 · Current (baseline)
"▲ REFINE | 30.0K Qi"
Affordable
Can't afford
What's in the game today. Reads as a generic action pill - the ▲ is small and could be confused with a sort/expand arrow. No explicit "upgrade" cue.
V2 · Level transition
"REFINE Lv 9 → 10 30.0K Qi"
Affordable
Can't afford
Adds explicit Lv N → N+1 to remove all doubt that this is an upgrade. Three subtly-tinted compartments (action / levels / cost) help the eye parse it. Cost is wider; may overflow on small phones.
V3 · Iconic upgrade badge
[▲] Refine to Lv 10 · 30.0K Qi
Affordable
Can't afford
Large brass icon-square on the left says "ACTION" at a glance; the body has the verb + target level on top, cost gold-hot below. Distinctive silhouette - reads as upgrade from across the screen. Unaffordable: cost flips to vermillion.
V4 · Progress-fill pill
"▲ REFINE / 30.0K Qi" with qi-progress aura
Affordable · 100%
Saving · 63%
An always-visible amber fill behind the text shows how close the player is to affording the next refine (qi/cost ratio). Reads as a "filling cup". The button stays semi-opaque when unaffordable so the player sees the progress filling up.
My pick: V3 + V4 combined - V3's iconic upgrade silhouette (left icon-square) with V4's embedded qi-progress fill. Most clearly reads as "this is an upgrade you're saving up to buy." Pick a variant and I'll ship it.

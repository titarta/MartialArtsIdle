Pure beads, no bar. Most distinctly "meditation cultivator" feel. Trade-off: no within-rung continuous progress (you only see discrete bead-lit states). Could lose the "approaching next milestone" feedback. Best paired with a tight countdown in the label.
My pick: V1 (brass scroll + medallion rungs). It matches the qi progress scroll vocabulary exactly (same brass border, same gold gradient, same chassis) so the focus meter reads as a SIBLING of the realm progress bar - cultivator surface family. The rung medallions light up as sacred milestones; the label splits current bonus (hero) and next bonus (caption) for clean hierarchy.

Achievement Detail . interaction study design: hung trophy plaque . MartialArtsIdle . 2026-05-27

Current pattern is a sticky drawer at the bottom of the scroll area. Tap a badge near the bottom and the drawer pops up directly over its neighbours, blocking what you just looked at and forcing a scroll. The drawer itself is the older flat-purple-with-X style, predating the Sanctum's brass / lacquer / vermillion vocabulary used everywhere else (tutorial card, Petition Tablet, REFINE pill, breakthrough button). Three replacements compared below; the Trophy Plaque (overlay modal-over-modal) is the recommendation.

Today Sticky drawer (broken)
Drawer is position: sticky; bottom: 0 inside the scroll area. Tapping a badge near the bottom of the grid puts the drawer right over neighbouring badges (see arrow). Design language is the older flat-purple modal style.
